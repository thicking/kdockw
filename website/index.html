KDockW

A customizable KDE Plasma 6 dock for Wayland, with parabolic zoom, grouped apps, and live previews of open and minimized windows.

Looking for a Latte Dock alternative for Plasma 6? KDockW is an independent spiritual successor based on Krema, with its own application identity and continued development. The original project’s Git history and copyright notices are preserved.

Made for Plasma on Wayland

  • Magnify dock icons with smooth parabolic zoom.
  • Group application windows, preview open and minimized windows, and switch between them from the dock.
  • Pin desktop launchers, reorder items, and drop files onto applications.
  • Use KDE-native settings, keyboard navigation, and Russian localization.

Origins and license
